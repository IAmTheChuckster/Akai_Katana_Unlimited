akatanaultd.zip is the full set with u4 replaced; u2, u23 and u24 are copied unchanged. On real hardware, keep a backup of your original u4 flash.

MiSTer: the .mra is for the ikacore CV1k core. Put it with your other arcade .mra files and akatanaultd.zip in your MAME ROM folder (games/mame). It loads akatanaultd.zip and checks for the Unlimited u4 (87a8a511). After you load a zip, the .mra also uses the CRC32s of the u2, u23 and u24 it found, so it always matches your set.
